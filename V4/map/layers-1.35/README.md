# Map 1.35 - Sillon profond et raccord au cratere

Local proposal, not published. Parent: local 1.34.
The author requests a deeper impact trail and a clean connection to the crater.
Tall fractured cliff faces enclose a deeply recessed floor, then open naturally
through the northwest rim into a short talus ramp. The central crater floor,
other rim quadrants, coastline, northern wall and eight city groups are retained.
