# Illustration Review

Original fan interpretations generated with the built-in image tool. Only
illustrations and the separate banner texture are generated; the native
897 x 1497 V4 frame, text, statistics and components are assembled separately.

Momo and Valazar remain the mandatory painterly references. Character identity
and weapons were checked against the official Final Fantasy X character page
and the visual references recorded in each art directory. These references are
identity guides, not compositions to copy or instructions to override the DA.

## Weapon And Framing Pass

- Brotherhood: rejected the first crystal-like silhouette; selected the long
  blue water blade with bubbles, its single reverse fork, ornate guard, wrapped
  yellow grip and tassel on the pommel ring.
- Auron: recomposed the sword vertically so the full weapon stays visible
  between the fixed stat rails; same protective expression and Luca setting.
- Jecht: broad dark blade, red inlay, linked chain, complete distinctive point.
- Blitzball: review the actual composed card, not only the unframed painting.
- Lulu keeps her Moogle plush in the illustration; Tome is only her existing
  gameplay weapon category. Yunalesca has no visible orb.

Source prompts, candidate versions and hashes live in `art-a/`, `art-b/`,
`art-c/` and `arenas/`. Parent selection is a production quality check, not an
assertion that the user has already approved these new images.
