# NieR / Replicant: art and profiles

Seven standalone illustrations produced with the built-in image generation tool.
Exact requests, local identity references, original output paths and individual
visual reviews are preserved in `art-c/*.request.json`. No card frame was generated.
Momo and Kaylis supply the painted style; licensed character sheets are identity
and costume references only, not composition or style templates.

## Adaptations

- Commander White: P5, LUXO, ANDROID, Sceptre for her command baton. Frontal,
  upright stance aboard the Bunker; support-oriented faces, not a damage dealer.
- Adult brother Nier: P4, NECRO, Tome (Grimoire Weiss), Lost Shrine approach.
- Kaine: P2, HEMATO, long-sword category with two serrated swords in the art.
- Devola Automata: P2/P3, PYRO, short sword; protective action by the Tower.
- Popola Automata: P3/P5, LUXO, short sword; helping at the Resistance Camp.
- Devola Replicant: P3/P4, AERO, Instrument; lute by the village fountain.
- Popola Replicant: P4/P5, LUXO, Sceptre; library and canonical crescent staff.

Crystals and role statistics are Kalistar adaptations, not claims about the
original game's elemental combat system. Nier and Kaine use the existing HUMAIN
game category; their canonical Replicant nature is not confused with ANDROID.
Commander and both pairs of twins use ANDROID. Automata remains faction `NieR`;
Replicant is a separate faction with an independent ATK synergy.

The two games feature different Devola/Popola units of the same model. The user
requested card versions, so `devola-nier` and `popola-nier` are intentionally
shared collection/deck identities across factions. This is a deck-rule choice,
not a statement that the units survived between the two stories.

Numeric faces are within principal-role bounds. Popola Automata D4 ATK was
corrected from a preparation draft of 128 to 112 before native assembly (P5 cap
120). No role, rule, approved existing card or published source was changed.
These checks do not assert simulated competitive win-rate balance.
