# Kalistar 4.6.42

Publishes the current atlas (map 1.28), including northern snow relief,
tropical archipelago and continental tips, the western volcanic plateau,
Vulkar, the abyss island and Cryptown. Cryptown's existing cross now stands
inside the dark hole; terrain and other city layers remain unchanged.

Vulkar and Cryptown are available in the desktop/phone city accordion with
matching markers and transparent thumbnail assets. Names and icons remain
editable separately in the PSD. Old map versions and runtime media preserved.

Validation: PSD/composite round-trip, native-to-WebP pixel identity, asset hashes,
map/deploy unit tests and browser checks at desktop, Razr, 320px and landscape.
