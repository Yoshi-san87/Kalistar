## Shared design

All five have white wolf fur, a black/dark forest-green Grivka military uniform,
an anatomical armored muzzle with frost vapor trailing backward, bright ice
Kalistels on the gloves, fluorescent cyan channels and a modern blue-lit belt.
Faces, builds, gestures, weapons and snowy settings vary.

The user's follow-up requested much stronger wolf-inspired weapon design.
Each weapon was edited individually while retaining the soldier and composition.
The sword/shield, claw-blade, rifle, jaw-hammer and bow use different combinations
of forged jaw or claw forms, pale fur trims and ice-blue light.

## Visual check

All retained generated images were viewed in full. The final five retain white fur,
shared uniform identity, backwards vapor and bright glove Kalistels.
The four armor-fur edits preserve their poses and grips. Nivra's composition
was rebuilt to show a centrally gripped bow, a single string and a separate
complete arrow with an icy point and white feather fletching. Long sword/barrel parts may leave
the frame rather than bending to fit, as requested. None has generated text,
card borders or interface elements. Artistic approval remains with the user.


## Revision 03: armor fur and archer rebuild

User requested more white fur on the other soldiers' armor and a complete
restart of the archer with personalized arrows. Nivra was regenerated in a
new standing patrol scene; the bow and separate arrow remain visually distinct.
The other four received tailored white fur collars, shoulder linings or coat
edgings while retaining their personalized weapon designs. Bright glove
Kalistels and belt lights remain visible. No native card or gameplay changed.

Built-in image_gen was used for all five revisions. Exact prompts are the
`prompts/*-03.txt` files. The earlier PNGs remain untouched.
