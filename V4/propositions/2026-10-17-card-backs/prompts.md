# Directions de generation (image_gen integre)

Contraintes communes : dos de carte Kalistar portrait 3:5, de face, sans
perspective ni mockup; cuivre et argent, metal travaille, texte exact KALISTAR,
aucun personnage ou chiffre de gameplay. Reference de matiere : dos actuel.

## 1 - Prisme souverain
Large sharp faceted full-spectrum rainbow diamond jewel in the exact center,
finely engraved copper/silver setting, deep near-black emerald enamel,
restrained angular borders, strong iconic silhouette at thumbnail size.

## 2 - Astrolabe d'Astralia
Concentric engraved copper astrolabe rings and fine silver navigation ticks,
dark midnight teal enamel, upright rainbow crystal at center, orbital paths
and star-shaped intersections, thin double copper rule, no generic galaxy.

## 3 - Grimoire des Fragments
Charcoal-black leather with subtle embossed angular scrollwork, weathered silver
corners, copper studs, central heraldic clasp with a small rainbow jewel,
dark garnet accents, generous leather field, no large astrolabe or giant jewel.

## 4 - Sceau de Chroma
Charcoal graphite geometric panel, recessed copper arcane circuitry, central
round metallic seal surrounding brilliant diamond-opal rainbow crystal like
the native card reference, selective cyan seams, architectural not steampunk.

## 5 - Vitrail des Kalistels
Elongated art-deco stained glass in copper/silver leading, teal/sapphire/jade/
amethyst/amber geometric wings around a perfectly upright rainbow crystal,
top and bottom vertices on one vertical axis, luminous glass, thin hammered
metal edge, no religious scene, no perspective.
